**What's new in Serafim 1.1.0**

**Major updates**
- **Notifications for posts.** Find out when a friend posts, when someone posts in a Circle you're in, and when someone comments on, replies to or Sparks your posts and comments. Tap a notification to go straight to the post.
- **Share posts into chats.** Send any post to a friend or a group. They see its heading and a preview picture, and tap it to open the post.
- **Draw Together, rebuilt.** Invitations now reach your friends even when Serafim is closed, and if someone's connection drops, the drawing is still there when they come back.
- **Automatic backups.** Back up your chats to Google Drive every week or every month, without having to remember (Settings → Backup & Restore).
- **Circles: leave, mute and choose notifications.** From a Circle's About tab you can leave it, mute it, or pick which notifications it sends you.

**More new things**
- Press and hold a chat to clear it, view the person's profile, or remove it from your list.
- Erasers in three sizes when drawing.
- An About section in Settings showing your app version.

**Improvements**
- Posts with several pictures show how many there are, and you can swipe through them.
- Clearer post buttons that show Spark and comment counts.
- The profile menu is easier to find, and it's on your profile page too.
- A new Wings mark, and smaller emoji in emoji-only messages.
- The Whispers tab is called Chats again.
- A slightly smaller download.

**Fixes**
- Follow and Following now stay correct on posts.
- New comments show up straight away, without restarting the app.
- Saving or cancelling a message edit no longer shows an error.
- Cropping a picture and then drawing on it keeps the crop.
