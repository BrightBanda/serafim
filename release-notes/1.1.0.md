**What's new in Serafim 1.1.0**

**Introducing Posts and the Feed**
- **Post to your profile.** Share a heading, some text and up to four pictures.
- **Your Feed.** A new Feed tab brings together posts from people you follow and from the Circles you've joined.
- **Sparks, comments and replies.** Spark the posts you like, comment on them, and reply to other people's comments.
- **Share posts into chats.** Send any post to a friend or a group. They see its heading and a preview picture, and tap it to open the post.

**Introducing Circles**
- **Communities around what you love.** Create a Circle with a name, a description, a category and its own rules, and choose who can join: anyone, by request, or by invitation only.
- **Find your people.** Discover Circles picked for you, or browse the popular ones by category.
- **Join in.** Join a Circle, or ask to join a private one, then post and talk with its members.
- **Run your Circle.** Owners and Overseers approve requests to join and pin important posts to the top.
- **Your Circle, your way.** Mute a Circle, choose which notifications it sends you, or leave it, all from its About tab.

**Notifications for what matters**
- Find out when a friend posts, when someone posts in a Circle you're in, and when someone comments on, replies to or Sparks your posts and comments. Tap a notification to go straight to the post.

**Also new**
- **Draw Together, rebuilt.** Invitations now reach your friends even when Serafim is closed, and if someone's connection drops, the drawing is still there when they come back.
- **Automatic backups.** Back up your chats to Google Drive every week or every month (Settings → Backup & Restore).
- Press and hold a chat to clear it, view the person's profile, or remove it from your list.
- Erasers in three sizes when drawing.
- An About section in Settings showing your app version.

**Improvements**
- The profile menu is easier to find, and it's on your profile page too.
- The notification bell is on every main page.
- A new Wings mark, and smaller emoji in emoji-only messages.
- The Whispers tab is called Chats again.
- A slightly smaller download.

**Fixes**
- Saving or cancelling a message edit no longer shows an error.
- Cropping a picture and then drawing on it keeps the crop.
